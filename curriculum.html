A week in a full-year course
  1. i

    Read

    Encounter a living work of literature.

  2. ii

    Narrate

    Tell what you encountered — before someone tells you what to think.

  3. iii

    Notice

    Find a word, image, pattern, question, or moment that lingers.

  4. iv

    Follow

    Trace it through the text.

  5. v

    Connect

    Bring it into conversation with history, philosophy, art, nature, or another writer.

  6. vi

    Write

    Say something true — and support it.

An oral narration at the beginning of a unit can become the seed of a thesis a student argues in their own words.
